Urgent signs.

When a clogged drain is an emergency.

  • More than one fixture is backing up. That points to the main line, not a single trap.
  • Sewage or dirty water is coming up through a floor drain or tub. Keep children and pets away and call right away.
  • A toilet overflows and will not stop. Shut the valve behind the bowl, then call.
  • Water is rising toward a finished basement or electrical equipment. Stay out of standing water near outlets and the panel.
  • The house has a single bathroom and no other place to flush.

Why Garfield drains clog in the first place.

Older houses in Belmont and Bogart Heights were often built with cast iron or clay drain lines. Those materials roughen with age, and grease, paper and roots catch on the rough spots. A line that has been cleared twice in a year is usually telling you something.

Hard water adds to the problem. The City of Garfield's 2018 water quality report lists hardness above the recommended range and says that hardness scales pipes. Scale narrows drain lines and gives grease a rough surface to cling to, so a kitchen line that fills with soap scum and scale clogs more often.

What to try, and what to avoid.

A plunger and a few minutes of patience clear many tub and sink clogs. A small hand auger can reach a hair clog in a bathroom sink. If the water does not move after a couple of tries, stop. Forcing a drain can crack an old trap or push a clog deeper.

Skip the liquid cleaners. They sit in a standing clog and eat at pipes, and the next person who opens the trap can be burned. For repeat problems, hydro jetting clears the walls of the pipe, and sewer line cleaning covers the main drain out to the street.
