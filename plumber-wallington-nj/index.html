Small lots, shared walls and what they do to plumbing.

Many Wallington homes are attached or close together, and a leak in one can reach a neighbor's wall. Tell the dispatcher if the water is coming from or going into another unit, and we will coordinate with the other party to shut off only what is needed.

Plumbing in older multi family buildings often shares a main drain or a water heater. A backup in a ground floor unit can start upstairs, so describe what is happening on all floors.

One thing worth checking this week.

Locate your main water shutoff and turn it off and on once. A valve that has not moved in years can seize, and you want to find out on a calm day instead of during a flood.

Keep a plumbing go-bag by the cellar stairs.

Wallington basements are close to the Passaic River, and small lots leave little space to work. A simple kit by the stairs helps: a flashlight, a bucket, towels, an adjustable wrench for the valve and a printed copy of this page. It takes five minutes to assemble.

If a pipe fails at night, the kit saves the scramble. Then the dispatcher can tell you what to turn off first, and our technician arrives to a cleaner, safer work area.
