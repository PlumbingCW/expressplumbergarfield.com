Email: contact@expressplumbergarfield.com, for non urgent questions. We answer as soon as we can. According to the Division of Consumer Affairs, you can verify any New Jersey plumber's license online before you call.

What to tell the dispatcher.

Give the street address, which floor the problem is on and what you see: water, sewage, no heat or a smell. Tell us if you have already shut off the main valve.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility first. Call us after the area is safe.
