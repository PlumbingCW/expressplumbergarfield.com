First five minutes.

When a burst pipe is an emergency.

  • Find the main shutoff and turn it clockwise until the water stops. In many Garfield homes it sits in the cellar near the meter.
  • Open the lowest faucet and a high one to drain the pipes and ease pressure.
  • Stay out of standing water near outlets, the panel or appliances. Shut off power at the breaker only if the panel is dry and reachable.
  • Photograph the damage before moving things, for your insurer.
  • Call the emergency line and tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from.

Burst pipe repair in Garfield, what we do on arrival.

We confirm the main is off, find the break and decide how to restore water the fastest. A split in a short section of copper can be cut out and replaced. A pipe that is corroded for several feet needs more than a patch.

If water is still coming in from the street side, we check the shutoff at the meter or the curb. In some homes the main valve is stuck, and fixing it is part of the visit. Tell us if you could not close yours. According to the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, burst pipe repair like this must be done by a licensed master plumber or someone working under one, so ask to see a license before you agree to a price.

Frozen pipes in a Bergen County winter.

Pipes in outside walls, unheated cellars and crawlspaces are the ones that freeze. If a faucet runs a trickle or not at all in a hard freeze, the pipe may be frozen but not yet split. Open the faucet, warm the area slowly with a hair dryer or space heater placed safely, and never use an open flame.

If you find a split, close the main before you thaw the pipe. A frozen pipe can hold back the flood until it thaws. Then everything arrives at once.

Water damage and insurance.

Most homeowner policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, but not the cost of repairing the pipe itself or damage from long term seepage. Call your agent early, photograph everything and keep damaged materials until the adjuster has seen them.

If water has soaked drywall or insulation, drying should start within a day or two. A restoration company handles that. We stop the leak and repair the pipe so drying can begin.
