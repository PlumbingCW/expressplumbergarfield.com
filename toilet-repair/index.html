Urgent signs.

When a toilet problem is an emergency.

  • The bowl is overflowing and the water will not stop rising.
  • Water leaks from the base onto the floor or through the ceiling below.
  • The only toilet in the house will not flush.
  • Sewage backs up into the toilet or tub, which points to a clog farther down the line.
  • The supply line is spraying or dripping steadily.

Toilet repair in Garfield, symptom by symptom.

Most toilet problems come from a short list of parts. A flapper that does not seal lets water leak from the tank to the bowl, and the fill valve refills over and over. A fill valve that sticks can overflow. A wax ring that has failed lets water seep out at the base.

Many Garfield bathrooms are small and on upper floors of two family houses, so a small leak can reach the ceiling below before anyone notices. If a stain shows up under a bathroom, a toilet is the first place to look. According to the City of Garfield's 2018 water quality report, hardness is above the recommended range, so scale on fill valves and rim jets is a common cause of weak flushes.

Choosing a replacement toilet.

When a toilet is cracked, constantly clogged or decades old, a new one makes sense. Look at the rough in distance, which is the measurement from the wall to the center of the drain. Most are twelve inches, but older bathrooms may differ, and a toilet that does not match will not fit.

Elongated bowls are more comfortable and round bowls save space. Taller bowls are easier for many adults to use. A modern low flow toilet uses much less water per flush than a toilet from the 1970s, which also helps on a metered bill.

Hard water and toilet parts.

Garfield's 2018 water quality report lists hardness above the recommended range. Scale builds on fill valves, flappers and the rim jets under the bowl. If your toilet flushes weakly, soaking the jets in vinegar can restore some flow, and a plumber can clean the rest.

Parts in hard water wear faster. Replacing a flapper every few years costs far less than the water wasted by a leaky one.
