The short version.

Water heater replacement in Garfield: what it covers.

Water heater replacement means draining the failed unit, disconnecting its water, gas or electric and venting connections, setting a new heater and testing it before it is put back in service. In Garfield that job usually happens in a basement, so stairs, headroom and the old flue all shape the plan.

A lot of Garfield blocks are lined with two and three family houses. That often means a heater serving one floor or a shared unit in the cellar, and we sort out which one is failing before anything is shut off. A tenant calling at midnight gets the same clear answer a homeowner does.

Urgent signs.

When a water heater is an emergency.

  • Water on the floor under the tank that keeps coming back after you wipe it. A tank that has rusted through cannot be patched.
  • Rust colored hot water along with a leak. Corrosion inside the tank has usually gone past the point of repair.
  • The relief valve is blowing off hot water or the tank is making loud banging sounds. Shut off the fuel and call right away.
  • A gas smell near the heater. Leave the building, call 911 or your gas utility from outside, and then call us. Do not flip switches on the way out.
  • No hot water at all in a home with young children, an elderly resident or a winter cold snap in the forecast.

If the leak is a drip from a pipe fitting rather than the tank body, the heater may be saved. That is why we look at water heater repair first on every visit and only quote a replacement when it is the better value.

Water heater leaking: where the water is really coming from.

Not every puddle means a dead tank. Water can come from the cold or hot supply fittings on top, the temperature and pressure relief valve, the drain valve at the bottom, a condensation line on a high efficiency unit or the tank itself.

Fittings and valves can be replaced for a modest part cost. Water that seeps from the bottom seam or out of the jacket means the steel shell has failed. We trace the source first, because replacing a tank that only needed a new relief valve wastes your money.

Repair or replace: how we decide.

Age is only one signal. A well maintained heater can outlast a neglected one by years, and the rating plate on the side of the tank carries a serial number that dates it.

Why Garfield water is hard on tanks.

The City of Garfield reported in its 2018 water quality report that it supplements its own wells with treated water bought from the Passaic Valley Water Commission. The two supplies blend at the Belmont Hill Tank, with the north end drawing mostly well water, the south end mostly Passaic Valley water and the middle a mix. The same report marks hardness as above the recommended range and says hardness scales pipes.

Scale is what builds on the bottom of a tank and on gas burner surfaces. It insulates the water from the flame, makes the tank noisy and shortens its life. If your kettle or showerhead scales up, your water heater is doing the same thing out of sight. A yearly drain and flush of a tank in Belmont or Bogart Heights is cheap insurance, and a failed heater is the moment to ask about a better sediment plan.

Tank, tankless or something in between.

A like for like tank swap is the fastest way back to hot water and usually the lowest upfront cost. A tankless water heater saves floor space and can heat water on demand, but it needs a gas line and venting sized for it, and older basements sometimes require upgrades first.

Most Garfield homes we see have the heater in the cellar next to the boiler or the laundry sink. Tell us how many people live upstairs and downstairs, how many showers run at once and how tight the stairs are. We will recommend a size and a type, and if you want to plan a larger project the water heater installation page covers new locations and conversions.

Licensed work and permits in Garfield.

New Jersey law limits plumbing work to a licensed master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or employees working under one, according to the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers. You can check any plumber through the Division of Consumer Affairs before you let them near your gas line.

Permit rules for a heater swap vary by town, so we ask the local construction office instead of guessing. The City of Garfield building department sits at 111 Outwater Lane, and its page lists plumbing inspections on Mondays and Wednesdays in the late morning. If your heater serves a rental unit, tell the landlord about the work and the permit.
