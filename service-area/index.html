Where we work.

Our base is in the city of Garfield, in Bergen County. From there, we serve Lodi, Elmwood Park, Wallington and Saddle Brook in Bergen County, and Passaic across the river in Passaic County.

Each town has its own water supplier, housing mix and flood history. We write about those details on the town pages so you can see what applies to your street.

Garfield neighborhoods.

Inside Garfield, we serve the neighborhoods of Belmont, Bogart Heights and Plauderville and the blocks around Midland Avenue, Passaic Street and River Drive. A call from any of them gets the same 24/7 response.

Before you call from a new town.

Have the street address, the nearest cross street and the floor or unit ready. Tell the dispatcher what you see and what you have already shut off. If you rent, say so, because the landlord or manager may need to approve the repair.

Those three details shorten the call and the drive. The dispatcher can then pick the closest free technician and give you a realistic arrival update for your street.

Why local dispatch matters.

A plumbing emergency is a race against water. The closer the technician starts, the less time the leak has. That is the plain reason this site is built around one base and a ring of nearby towns, instead of a long list of cities we reach only on paper.

If your town is not listed, call anyway. We tell you honestly whether we can reach you in a reasonable time, and we do not accept a call we cannot answer well.

How dispatch reaches each town.

Our technicians start from Garfield, so the closest towns see the shortest drives. Lodi, Wallington and Elmwood Park share a border with the city. Saddle Brook sits just north, and Passaic is across the river.

Traffic on Route 46, Route 17 and the river bridges changes the real drive time through the day. We give you an arrival update when we dispatch, and we never promise a fixed arrival window.

What every call includes.

The same standard applies in every town. A dispatcher answers, a technician is sent as soon as one is free, and you hear a clear price before any work starts. If a job needs a permit, we tell you which office handles it for your town.

For services, start with drain cleaning, water heater replacement or burst pipe repair. The full service list covers everything else, and a phone call works at any hour.
